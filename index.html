How DrainTrace Works

A seamless six-step lifecycle that turns daily kitchen routines into independently sealed compliance certainty.

1
STEP 1 Day 1 Onboarding

SETUP

Establish your estate baseline in the DrainTrace cloud.

Load:
  • Site details (locations, trading hours, responsible managers)
  • Asset Register (grease traps, GRUs, biological dosing systems)
  • Maintenance contracts (service providers, scheduled service intervals)
2
STEP 2 Under 2 Minutes Daily

CAPTURE

Kitchen teams complete the rapid guided mobile capture.

Kitchen teams complete:
  • Photo capture (visual condition of separator & kitchen drainage)
  • Fill-level reading (quick gauge check or dip measurement)
  • Compliance checklist (strainers emptied, dosing unit active)
Designed to complete within two minutes.
3
STEP 3 Maintenance Visits

CONTRACTOR LOGGING

Seamlessly verify external contractor servicing and grease removal.

Record service visits through:
  • Invoice upload (instant digital photo or PDF capture)
  • QR scanning (on-site contractor check-in and confirmation)
  • Contractor data (automated pump-out quantities and disposal records)
4
STEP 4 Automated Engine

VERIFY

The core intelligence layer evaluates all submissions in real time.

Verification Engine checks:
  • Contracts (adherence to required service specifications)
  • Service intervals (flagging early or delayed maintenance)
  • Historical records (pattern consistency across past trading weeks)
  • Compliance patterns (detecting anomalies or fabricated inputs)
5
STEP 5 Append-Only Store

SEAL

Verified records become tamper-evident compliance evidence.

Integrity Guarantee:
  • Cryptographic hashing locks timestamps and photo evidence
  • Append-only architecture prevents retroactive record alteration
  • Permanent, audit-grade proof ready for inspection scrutiny
6
STEP 6 Instant Distribution

SHARE

Share verified compliance evidence with external authorities effortlessly.

Generate reports for:
  • EHOs (during scheduled or unannounced kitchen inspections)
  • Water companies (verifying compliance with Section 111)
  • Insurers (protecting against sewer backup and business interruption claims)
  • Franchise partners (enforcing network-wide operational standards)
